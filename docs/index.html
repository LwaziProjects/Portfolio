It needs periodic calibration to stay accurate. I took part in calibrating the trackside acoustic cabinets and wayside hut hardware, configured speaker set-up points and sensitivity thresholds, and confirmed sensor performance in the trending database afterwards.

    RailBAM Wayside Software, Process Monitor, acoustic sensor calibration

    Full notes

    • Worked with Process Monitor for session control and fault handling during calibration
    • Observed RailBAM Trending Database output to confirm correct sensor performance post-calibration

    Key challenges

    • Detecting faint acoustic fault signatures in a high-noise trackside environment

  • Large-sparse historical consistent neural networks for spatially resolved fault detection and localisation in photovoltaic arrays

    The sparsity mask is derived from the array’s electrical wiring graph, the model trains on healthy operating data only, and faults are located from the spatial geometry of the prediction-error residuals. It is being validated against a component-level Simscape Electrical model of an 80 kW array.

    MATLAB, Simulink, Simscape Electrical

    Full notes
    • The array model is built programmatically: a MATLAB script places and wires the Simscape Electrical blocks. MATLAB R2026a dropped the Specialized Power Systems library, so the model moved to the Simscape Electrical foundation library.
    • Planned fault library: line-to-line, line-to-ground, open circuit, degradation, shading and bypass diode faults, in series-parallel and total-cross-tied layouts.
    • Weather forcing from SAURAN station data, with external validation on the GPVS-Faults dataset.
    • Benchmarks planned against dense and random-mask HCNNs, an LSTM autoencoder, supervised classifiers and the string-current residual method.
    • Groundwork: a review of eleven papers on digital twins in power systems, predictive analytics in smart grids, and detecting false data injection, remote tripping command injection, system reconfiguration and denial-of-service attacks.
